## Reference eras

### A. JTR original — 2007

Sources:
- https://tig.fandom.com/wiki/Death_Worm
- https://www.tigsource.com/2007/06/27/death-worm/

Verified high-level observations:
- Developer listed as JTR.
- Released in 2007.
- Windows, single-player, keyboard.
- Core fantasy: control a giant sandworm that spends much of its time underground, leaps out of the sand and eats surface targets.
- TIGSource described timing/strategy around surviving repeated human retaliation as a notable part of the fun.

Confidence: high for these high-level facts.

### B. PlayCreek Flash-era version — around 2011

Useful source:
- https://www.kongregate.com/en/games/playcreek/death-worm

Verified observations from the listing:
- 15 levels.
- 30 enemy types.
- leaderboards and achievements.
- keyboard controls include acceleration/turning, fireball, Nitro and pause.
- core targets include humans, animals, cars, tanks, aircraft and helicopters.

Additional community documentation:
- https://flashgaming.fandom.com/wiki/Death_Worm

Community-documented systems include:
- level kill requirements and some timed objectives;
- upgrades after levels;
- escalating hostile enemies;
- Nitro and Fireball power-ups;
- upgrade categories such as size, speed, skin strength, Nitro duration and Fireball level.

Treat community-wiki details as useful but secondary evidence.

### C. Current PlayCreek mobile game — checked 2026

Primary source:
- https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.playcreek.DeathWorm_Free&hl=en

Verified from the current Google Play listing:
- Campaign, Survival, Blitz and Mini Games are described.
- XP and coins are used for progression/unlocking.
- multiple locations and worm types.
- worm upgrade/level-up systems.
- over 40 enemy types are claimed.
- current update notes mention unique super abilities for worms.
- current update notes mention additional worms/location, animated worms, a newer UI, gamepad support, achievements and cloud save.
- listing classifies the game as single-player, stylized/stylized-realistic, monster/destruction oriented.

Official PlayCreek:
- https://www.playcreek.com/
- describes the game at a high level around eating, growing, combo scoring, special power-ups and survival.

## Important research rule

Do not merge A, B and C into one feature list without labeling the era.

The new game should borrow the enduring loop:
- underground movement;
- breach attack;
- prey/destruction;
- escalating retaliation;
- score/combo;
- power progression;

while building its own presentation and new two-sided Worm/Hunter design.
